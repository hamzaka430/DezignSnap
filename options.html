Quick Operations & Shortcuts

• Alt + S: Instant Area Snip (drag rectangle to zoom & analyze).

• Alt + A: Full webpage screenshot solve.

• Alt + P: Open Side Panel (100% immune to Google Meet, Teams, and Zoom Tab Sharing — works live while you share).

• Alt + H: Instant Stealth Toggle (hides/restores on-screen toggle dock & panel to prevent screen share capture).

• Escape: Instant Panic Dismiss (closes AI answer panel immediately in 0ms).

• Right-Click Menu: Highlight any text on any webpage and select "Ask AI about selection" for instant explanation without snipping.

• File Access: To snip local PDFs/images, go to chrome://extensions, click Details on this extension, and toggle "Allow access to file URLs".
