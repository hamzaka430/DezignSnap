Privacy Policy — DezignSnap

Last Updated: October 2026 | Canonical URL: https://dezignsnap-privacy.hamzaka549.workers.dev/privacy-policy

Zero Data Collection & Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) Architecture:
DezignSnap is built with privacy as its primary foundation. We do not operate any middleman or proxy servers. We collect zero personal data, sell zero user data, and include no analytics, no tracking, and no telemetry. All network calls occur directly between your browser and your self-selected AI provider API using your personal API key (or stay 100% offline on your device when using local Ollama).

1. What DezignSnap Does

DezignSnap is an on-page visual assistant Chrome Extension (Manifest V3) that enables users to snip webpage regions, capture visible viewports, or submit highlighted text queries to receive instant visual analysis powered by Google Gemini, OpenAI (GPT-4o), Groq, OpenRouter, or local offline Ollama.

2. Information We Collect

We (the extension developer, Muhammad Hamza / Dezignwise) collect NO personal data from you.

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, or device IDs.
  • No Browsing History: We do not track, record, or transmit the websites you visit.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: DezignSnap contains no Google Analytics, no telemetry trackers, no cookies, and no third-party tracking scripts.
  • No Advertising: DezignSnap contains zero advertising code, trackers, or monetization scripts.

3. What Is Stored Locally on Your Device

All user configuration data is stored exclusively in your browser's local sandbox storage (chrome.storage.local). It never syncs to external servers:

  • Your API Key(s): Stored locally on your device to authorize direct API requests to your chosen provider (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Groq, or OpenRouter). Keys are never transmitted to us or any intermediary.
  • Active Provider & Model Selection: Which AI engine you have selected.
  • Instruction Presets & Personas: Custom system prompt templates you create or select (e.g., General Explainer, Quiz & MCQ Solver, ELI5, Roast My UI).
  • UI Preferences: Toolbar dock position (Left, Center, Right), collapsed/expanded state, and reasoning mode (Direct vs. Thinking).
  • Local Session History: Q&A history entries with compressed image thumbnails, used solely to display your in-tab chat transcript and generate client-side PDF exports. Stored with a strict FIFO cap and clearable at any time.

4. What Data Is Shared With Third Parties

Data is transmitted strictly upon your explicit request (e.g., clicking the Snip Area button, clicking Full Page Solve, or submitting a question). Data is transmitted directly from your browser to your selected AI provider:

  • Google Gemini: Dispatched to generativelanguage.googleapis.com using your Gemini API key. Subject to Google Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI: Dispatched to api.openai.com using your OpenAI secret key. Subject to OpenAI Privacy Policy.
  • Groq: Dispatched to api.groq.com using your Groq API key. Subject to Groq Privacy Policy.
  • OpenRouter: Dispatched to openrouter.ai using your OpenRouter key. Subject to OpenRouter Privacy Policy.
  • Local Ollama: Dispatched to your local computer (http://localhost:11434). 100% private and offline; zero data leaves your machine.

We do not act as a gateway, proxy, or intermediary. We never see, log, or have access to your prompts, screenshots, or responses.

5. Permissions Requested & Technical Justifications

  • host_permissions: <all_urls> — Required for On-Page Visual Assistant HUD: DezignSnap injects a floating toolbar and interactive drag-to-snip overlay so you can select and analyze visual content on whatever webpage you choose to inspect. Because users research across arbitrary websites, access to <all_urls> is essential. No background monitoring or auto-capture occurs; screenshots and queries are triggered strictly by explicit user actions.
  • activeTab — Temporary Screenshot Capture: Allows the background service worker to capture the visible viewport (captureVisibleTab) solely when you invoke a snip or solve action.
  • storage & unlimitedStorage — Local Offline Data: Saves your API keys, preferences, and session Q&A history with image thumbnails locally on your computer. Storing session image thumbnails can exceed default 10MB quotas over extended research sessions, necessitating unlimitedStorage.
  • contextMenus — Right-Click Convenience: Provides the right-click "Ask AI about selection" menu item for highlighted text.

6. How to Delete Your Data

You have complete control over all data stored by DezignSnap:

  1. Clear Session History: Open DezignSnap Settings (or right-click the extension icon → Options), scroll to the Session History & PDF Reports section, and click "Clear Session History". All stored conversation records and thumbnails are permanently erased.
  2. Remove API Keys: Open Settings, clear the API key input fields, and click Save.
  3. Complete Data Erasure: Uninstalling the extension from Chrome (right-click icon → "Remove from Chrome...") instantly and permanently purges all local storage, keys, preferences, and cached session data from your device.

7. Security Practices

DezignSnap enforces modern Chrome Extension Manifest V3 security standards:

  • Strict Content Security Policy (no eval(), no remote scripts, no inline event handlers).
  • Clickjacking defense: all interactive handlers verify event.isTrusted to prevent malicious host scripts from simulating clicks.
  • Isolated world execution: content scripts run in isolated contexts, preventing page scripts from accessing extension state.
