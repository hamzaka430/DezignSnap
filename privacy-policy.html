Privacy Policy — DezignSnap

Last updated: September 2026

What this extension does

DezignSnap lets you capture a screenshot of the currently visible part of a webpage, select a specific region (Smart Zoom), or highlight text and send it directly to your chosen AI engine (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenAI, OpenRouter, or local Ollama) to receive instant, grounded explanations.

What data we collect

We (the extension developer) do not operate any intermediate servers and do not collect, receive, or store any of your data ourselves. All data handled by this extension stays either on your device or is dispatched directly from your browser to your selected AI provider (or processed 100% locally when using Ollama).

What is stored locally on your device

  • Your API key(s) for Groq, Gemini, OpenAI, and/or OpenRouter (used only to authenticate requests to the selected provider's API directly from your browser).
  • Which provider engine is currently active.
  • Your saved instruction presets and personas.
  • A local session history of your Q&A exchanges and compressed thumbnails, used only to display your session transcript and generate client-side PDF reports. This can be cleared at any time from Settings.

This data is stored using the browser's built-in extension storage (chrome.storage.local) and is never synced to external analytics or ad trackers.

What is sent to third parties

When you invoke DezignSnap, the screenshot or selected text and your instruction are sent directly from your browser to your selected provider: Groq (api.groq.com), Google Gemini (generativelanguage.googleapis.com), OpenAI (api.openai.com), or OpenRouter (openrouter.ai) using your own API key. If you select Ollama, no data leaves your local machine.

We do not intercept, log, or have access to your data or tokens at any point.

Permissions this extension requests, and why

  • storage & unlimitedStorage — to save your API keys, presets, and session thumbnails locally.
  • activeTab — to allow the extension to capture a screenshot of the tab you are viewing when you trigger an action.
  • contextMenus — to provide the right-click "⚡ Ask AI about selection" convenience menu for highlighted text.
  • host_permissions — required to display the floating HUD on pages and communicate directly with AI provider APIs.

Your controls

You can clear your session history at any time from the Settings page. You can remove or replace your API keys at any time. Uninstalling the extension completely purges all local storage.
