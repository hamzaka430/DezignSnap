Welcome to Page Helper AI 👋

Capture a screenshot of any webpage and get instant AI help — understand what's on the page, or locate the option you're looking for. Setup takes about two minutes.

1

Set your API key

Open Settings and enter your OpenAI or Gemini API key. It's stored only in your browser.

2

Customize your instruction

A default instruction is ready to go, but you can create your own presets for different tasks.

3

Click the button on any page

Look for "🧠 Solve with AI" in the bottom-right corner — click it and get your answer.
